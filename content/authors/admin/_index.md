I am a Principal Project Scientist at the [Centre for Responsible AI (CeRAI)](https://cerai.iitm.ac.in), [Wadhwani School of Data Science and AI (WSAI)](https://wsai.iitm.ac.in), [IIT Madras](https://iitm.ac.in/). My research is in theoretical machine learning, grounded in theoretical computer science, and extends into responsible AI and computer vision. I design algorithms with provable guarantees, often going beyond worst-case analysis, and I use these tools to study when an AI system can be trusted: whether it is fair, whether it can forget data on request, and whether it is safe across languages and cultures.

Previously, I was a Visiting Assistant Professor in the [Department of Artificial Intelligence](https://ai.iith.ac.in) at [IIT Hyderabad](https://iith.ac.in), where I taught four courses, and a Postdoctoral Associate at the [Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard](https://www.broadinstitute.org/), where I worked on deep learning for genomic structural variant discovery. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from [Northwestern University](https://www.northwestern.edu/), where I was a member of the [CS theory group](https://theory.cs.northwestern.edu/), advised by [Prof. Konstantin Makarychev](https://konstantin.makarychev.net/) and [Prof. Aravindan Vijayaraghavan](https://users.eecs.northwestern.edu/~aravindv/). During my Ph.D., I interned at [Adobe Research](https://research.adobe.com). I received my B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering from [IIT Kanpur](https://iitk.ac.in/), with a minor in Physics.
